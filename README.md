## Who I am

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering student at St. Joseph's College of Engineering, Chennai, working across both sides of the stack — microcontrollers, servos, and sensors on the hardware side; web apps, dashboards, and firmware tooling on the software side. Most of what I build lately sits at the intersection of embedded systems and AI: path trackers, control dashboards, predictive health monitors, and vehicles that sense a lot more than they're given credit for.

## What I'm building

### 🌊 SmartScout
AUV mission-control dashboard for deep-sea mineral exploration, built alongside the Smart Scout OBN swarm concept pitched at Smart India Hackathon — passive, free-falling sensor nodes that map polymetallic sulphides and cobalt crusts with zero benthic footprint.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Firebase RTDB` `Leaflet`

Deep Ocean Research Observation Probe — a module that mounts onto an existing submarine, scans the seafloor for metals as the sub moves, and turns the readings into a live 3D topology heatmap.

`Scroll-driven UI` `CSS State Machine`

</td>

Leader-follower robotic arm with potentiometer-based closed-loop feedback and a full web dashboard — live monitor, record, playback, and presets. One of two builds I'm actively deep in right now.

`ESP32` `SG90 Servos` `Closed-loop Control`

An iPod-classic-style Spotify browser with a genuinely functional click wheel, running on OAuth PKCE and the Spotify Web Playback SDK. Also actively in progress.

`OAuth PKCE` `Web Playback SDK`

Predictive health monitoring for elevator drive systems — reads voltage and current waveforms across the AC input, rectifier, DC bus, inverter, and motor stages to catch degradation before it becomes a failure.
